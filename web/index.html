What you are looking at

A 3D model of the Mississippi Sound — Lake Borgne to Dauphin Island. The mainland waterline is the OpenStreetMap coastline, so the bays, bayous and river mouths are the real ones, and the islands follow their OpenStreetMap shorelines (Cat, West/East Ship, Horn, Petit Bois, Dauphin, Deer, Round). Open-shelf depths come from the GEBCO 2024 grid; inside the Sound, the bays and the lagoons they are still a procedural stand-in. Neither is NOAA survey data. North is the mainland from Pearlington through Gulfport and Pascagoula to Bayou La Batre. Magenta ticks are the Louisiana and Alabama lines.

Moving around

Drag to pan east–west and north–south. Scroll to zoom. Right-drag (or two fingers) to tilt. Arrow keys pan once the map has focus.

Not a photograph

The surface is a heightfield. Each PNG tile stores depth in its RGB values (Mapbox terrain-RGB encoding). The GPU lifts every vertex from that depth and tints it by depth (hypsometric colour, hillshade, optional contours).

Zoom levels 6–14 are the same idea as a web map: more tiles, finer ground sample. They are not extra pictures of the same view.

Where the depths come from

South of the barrier chain the floor is the GEBCO 2024 global grid, clipped to this chart and bundled with the build so the viewer runs with the network unplugged. GEBCO's 15 arc-second cells are about 460 m across, which is too coarse to resolve the Sound, the bays and the lagoons — those are still a procedural stand-in, and the two are blended across the shelf edge.

The surface is therefore modified GEBCO, not the published grid. It is not for navigation, and it is not NOAA survey data — it must not be presented as unaltered NOAA bathymetry or as National Bathymetric Source.

Ocean overlay

A snapshot HYCOM surface-current field can be drawn as particles. Motion is visually exaggerated and is not a time stepper. NDBC stations appear as wind barbs. This viewer is not an official NOAA product. Surface currents courtesy of the HYCOM consortium (dataset id when present). Buoy observations are NOAA NDBC open data.

Aircraft overlay

Live public ADS-B positions over this box can appear as heading marks. Data from The OpenSky Network, with adsb.lol as fallback. Not for navigation. Not a snapshot; this layer is empty with the network unplugged. Do not infer endorsement by OpenSky or adsb.lol.
